# Research integrity

- Preserve pinned upstream files and source manifests byte for byte. Verify recorded hashes; do not silently refresh sources from a moving branch.
- Distinguish published claims, released-code behavior, direct observations and project decisions. Cite the relevant source or executable evidence.
- Record only checks actually performed and results actually observed. State unresolved questions and execution limits explicitly.
- Do not modify, repartition, deduplicate or impute research data without an explicit, documented methodological decision.
- Keep Test out of model selection and setting exploration. Freeze the protocol before final evaluation; choose explanatory examples from Train.
- Do not treat exact string non-overlap as participant/game independence or infer identities from trait profiles.
- Document method changes and experimental rules before using them. Keep synthetic technical fixtures distinct from research configurations and results.
- Limit predictive, causal and cognitive interpretations to what the evidence supports.
- Resolve execution paths from the project root. Keep environments, caches, large artifacts and personal operational records out of Git.
- Maintain English research documentation, reproducible commands, source versions and evidence links. If generated material changes, update its generator or publication export and verify the affected output.
